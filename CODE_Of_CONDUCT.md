# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in Open-Source Club (alpha)'s community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community — especially one that's friendly to students writing their first lines of open-source code.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Being patient with newcomers — everyone was a beginner once
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Trolling, and deliberately disruptive or inflammatory remarks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Dismissing or belittling someone for their skill level or questions
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a student/academic setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any club meetings or events — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the club in public spaces (e.g. using an official email or social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at a hackathon or conference).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Club maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to [maintainer email / form link]. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and all maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction** — Private, written warning for minor/first-time inappropriate behavior, with clarity around why it was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with those involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period.
4. **Permanent Ban** — A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community, for a pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression.
